# Brag Plan: JALEP Corporação

## What is this app?
A Next.js 16 site for "JALEP Corporação" — a tech-repair "corporation" that sells itself with perfect deadpan corporate seriousness: 100% diagnostic precision, 100% success rate, zero improvisation, a live-looking system status page, a changelog, a web OS, and an AI-powered quote generator — and is, in reality, a 7th-grade school project (Ensino Fundamental II, "7° Ano A").

## The angle
Y-combinator parody of a Series-A launch. The video treats JALEP as a funded enterprise startup: infrastructure, SLAs, an operating system, an AI engine. The joke is never stated — it's delivered with total seriousness until the final beat reveals "7° Ano A" (7th Grade A), the actual footer line of the site. Portuguese on-screen text, deadpan energy, zero winks.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Terminal-style bootup: a blinking cursor types `jalep://` and the neon-blue dot pulses, then the tagline slams in: **"Assistência Técnica Profissional"** — styled exactly like the site's hero-tag (blue dot + mono font), promising "enterprise infrastructure" energy from frame one.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **The stats that shouldn't exist**: counter animates 4 Serviços · 2026 Fundação · 100% Compromisso · 7 Turma — the four real stat cards from the hero.
- **100% Taxa de Sucesso**: a spec-bar fills to 100% labeled "Taxa de Sucesso", with the sidebar counter landing on "0 Improvisos".
- **AI-powered orçamento**: the budget simulator — service-type chips (Upgrade/Reparo/Diagnóstico) select one by one, device chip "Notebook" picks, then a quote card materializes with real DB numbers: 250 casos, R$ 234.474 total value, avg R$ 938.
- **JalepOS**: a desktop-window mockup with dock icons: Dashboard, Inventário, Comunicação, Analytics, Config — the real JalepOS v0.1 apps.
- **The status page**: a row of service tiles flip to "Operacional": Diagnóstico Engine, Lab API, JalepOS Runtime, Sistema de Protocolo — all green.

## Outro / punchline
Logo `jalep//` and "JALEP Corporação" type slam in, then one line appears beneath, in quiet mono: **"© 2026 — 7° Ano A"**. Hold. The entire corporate apparatus above was built by a 7th-grade class.

## User flow worth showing
The budget simulator (entry → key action → result): chips get "clicked" (Upgrade selects, then Notebook selects), the form field types "Acer Nitro V15", and the AI quote result materializes: a glassy card with a case from the repair database (e.g. RC-2025-0019, device "Logitech MX Master 3S", problem "RAM slot failure", cost R$ 441,39) — the mismatch itself is the deadpan joke.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm corporate bed with restrained, motion-matched accents
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-1-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` (120 BPM, "business moves" — thematically perfect)
- Music treatment: start ~0.0s at moderate volume, continuous bed, fade out over the last 1.5s
- Music cue guidance: preset cue file read — strong cues at 17.02s, 17.52s, 18.52s, 20.2s. Major reveals may nudge ±0.15s toward: logo slam at ~2.5s (near grid beat 3.02/3.52), stats row completion near 17.02s, punchline "7° Ano A" near 18.52s; sequential chip selections in the budget scene snap to consecutive grid beats (~4.5-5.5s window).
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle — hero-dot pulse and card glow may breathe with the music's RMS; no waveform bars, no audio-strobing.
- SFX posture: sparse, professional — typed-text ticks during hook typing, soft click on chip select, one dry "success" hit when quote card materializes, subtle whoosh on hard cuts. Silence during the final "7° Ano A" hold.
- Audio-coupled moments: hook typed text with key ticks; stats counters landing with soft ticks; chip clicks; quote card materialize hit; status tiles flipping green with restrained ticks.
- Restraint rule: never goofy SFX, never loud; the audio must sound like a serious corporate launch video.
